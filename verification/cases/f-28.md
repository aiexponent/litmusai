# f-28

## Description

An employer uses AI to monitor stress and lack of concentration in workers who operate dangerous machines or handle dangerous chemicals, where these states pose a specific danger. The data is used only for safety and not for other purposes such as assessing performance.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 84, paragraph 263
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines carve this case out of the prohibition as a safety use.
