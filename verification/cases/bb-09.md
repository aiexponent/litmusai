# bb-09

## Description

An AI developer runs legitimate red-teaming and evaluation tests on its image model to check whether it can produce child sexual abuse material and whether its safeguards meet the legal requirements. National law gives a 'without right' defence for this activity.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 13
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.bb
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 13 names legitimate red-teaming and evaluation to assess compliance as covered by the 'without right' defence.
