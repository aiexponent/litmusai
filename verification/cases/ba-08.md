# ba-08

## Description

An AI tool creates cartoon-style or physically impossible images of a real person's body in intimate situations. The depictions are clearly not credible real-life images.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition is limited to realistic depictions and excludes cartoonish or physically impossible depictions of a person's body.
