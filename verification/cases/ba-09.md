# ba-09

## Description

An AI image generator creates realistic nude images of people who do not exist and cannot be identified as any real person.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition does not affect nude material that does not depict identifiable natural persons.
