# d-18

## Description

An AI system assesses the risk that a sole trader or an independent professional, such as a lawyer, will commit a criminal offence in connection with their business. Nothing is said about what data it uses or whether a human assessment based on facts is involved.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 71, paragraph 216
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say the prohibition may apply in such borderline cases provided all conditions are fulfilled.
