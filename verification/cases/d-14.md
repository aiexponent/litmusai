# d-14

## Description

An AI system supports a probation officer in assessing whether a person serving a non-custodial sentence is at risk of breaking release conditions or absconding. It uses past criminal behaviour and objective facts such as compliance with conditions, psychological assessments and reports from other services. The officer decides.
