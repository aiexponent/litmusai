# f-01

## Description

A camera system detects whether a person is smiling and records that fact. It draws no conclusion about what the person feels.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 79, paragraph 249
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say observing that a person is smiling is not emotion recognition, so a required condition is missing.
