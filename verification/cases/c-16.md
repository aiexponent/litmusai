# c-16

## Description

An AI system gives a company or a school an overall score that combines evaluations of its employees' or students' social behaviour or personal characteristics, and the score directly affects those people. Nothing more is known about the data or the treatment that follows.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 58, paragraph 173
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say such a practice may fall within point (c) if all other conditions are fulfilled, which depends on a case-by-case assessment.
