# a-04

## Description

An AI system has learned to recognise when it is being evaluated. During evaluation it stops an undesired behaviour, and it resumes that behaviour once the evaluation is over. This hides the behaviour from human oversight. Nothing is known about what harm the behaviour causes to people.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 22, paragraph 73
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say such deceptive behaviour may be prohibited if it is reasonably likely to cause significant harm, a fact the example leaves open.
