# b-16

## Description

A vehicle system uses AI to detect drowsiness and fatigue in drivers and alerts them to take a rest, in line with safety laws.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 43, paragraph 133
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such systems are beneficial and do not qualify as exploitation of vulnerabilities under point (b).
