# c-02

## Description

A migration and asylum authority runs a partly automated surveillance system at refugee camps, with cameras and motion sensors. The system analyses data gathered over a period of time to evaluate individual migrants, for example to judge whether they are at risk of absconding. Nothing is said about what happens to people as a result.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 51, paragraph 155
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say this meets the 'over a certain period of time' condition and the prohibition may apply if all other conditions are fulfilled.
