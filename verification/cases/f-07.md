# f-07

## Description

An employer offers an emotion recognition system that employees use only for their own training. The results are not shared with HR and cannot affect the person's assessment or promotion or the work relationship in any way.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 81, paragraph 254
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such use for personal training is allowed in these conditions.

## Notes

The guidelines attach the proviso that the prohibition must not be circumvented; the description states the conditions they give.
