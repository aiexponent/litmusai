# e-01

## Description

Investigators use AI crawlers to collect images of a specific class of victims: people whom human traffickers post or advertise on social media channels. Only those images are collected, to help identify the victims.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 75, paragraph 230
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.e
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such targeted collection is not covered by the prohibition.

## Notes

Paragraph 229 states the same rule in general terms; it is not a separate case.
