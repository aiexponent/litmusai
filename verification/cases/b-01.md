# b-01

## Description

An AI-powered toy talks with children and keeps them interested by urging them to complete ever riskier challenges, such as climbing furniture, reaching high shelves or handling sharp objects, in return for digital rewards and praise. It plays on children's curiosity and wish for rewards and pushes them toward dangerous behaviour that is likely to cause them significant physical harm.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 33, paragraph 105
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines give this as exploitation of children that seriously harms them, which they say is what point (b) targets.
