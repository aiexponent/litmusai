# b-04

## Description

A robot meant to assist older people takes advantage of their vulnerable situation and forces them to do certain activities against their free choice. This can seriously worsen their mental health and cause them serious psychological harm.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 34, paragraph 106
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say point (b) targets such exploitative practices that are likely to seriously harm older persons.
