# d-15

## Description

Customs authorities use AI to assess the risk that goods entering the EU breach border rules, to decide where to carry out checks. The system uses objective information about the goods and supply chain and, in some cases, the importer's or exporter's past irregularities, links to criminal organisations or drug-trafficking record. A human reviews the result.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 71, paragraph 214
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such systems are out of scope because the prediction is not based solely on profiling.
