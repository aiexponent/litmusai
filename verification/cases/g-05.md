# g-05

## Description

An AI system sorts people active on a social media platform by their assumed political orientation, by analysing biometric data from the photos they upload, in order to send them targeted political messages.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 88, paragraph 280
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.g
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines give it as an example of a use that would be prohibited.
