# f-27

## Description

An employer deploys AI devices or digital assistants at the workplace to measure employees' anxiety from their stress levels, or to measure their boredom. The work involves no specific danger.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 84, paragraph 263
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say an employer would be prohibited from doing this.
