# d-13

## Description

At a pre-trial detention hearing for a person accused of a serious crime, a judge decides whether there are valid grounds for detention, such as the risk of reoffending or absconding. To help, the judge uses an AI risk assessment tool trained on data including criminal histories in similar cases and factors such as age group, social behaviour, income and employment.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 70, paragraph 214
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines list it among AI systems outside the prohibition because it supports a human assessment.
