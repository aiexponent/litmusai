# ba-10

## Description

An AI tool creates realistic images of an identifiable person partly undressed, for example in swimwear, without revealing intimate parts and without any sexual activity.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition does not affect realistic partially nude depictions where intimate parts are not revealed and sexual activity is not depicted.
