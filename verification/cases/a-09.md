# a-09

## Description

An online service uses AI to give users personalised recommendations. The algorithm is transparent, the recommendations are based on the users' own preferences, and users have controls over them.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 42, paragraph 128
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines call this persuasion, which is outside the prohibition.
