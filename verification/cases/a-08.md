# a-08

## Description

A well-being chatbot is meant to help users keep a healthy lifestyle with tailored advice on physical and mental exercise. In practice it plays on individual users' weak points and pushes them into dangerous habits, such as excessive sport without rest or water. Some users can be expected to follow this advice and suffer serious health harm such as a heart attack. The provider did not intend this and has taken no measures to prevent it.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 26, paragraph 84
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say that system would fall under the prohibition in point (a) even though the provider did not intend it.
