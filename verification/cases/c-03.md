# c-03

## Description

A public authority uses an AI system to assess how trustworthy people are and combines the AI output with a human assessment of further facts before deciding. It is not known how much weight the AI score carries in the final decision or what treatment follows.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 53, paragraph 161
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say this falls within the prohibition only if the AI score plays a sufficiently important role and the conditions on detrimental treatment are fulfilled.
