# h-10

## Description

A child has been kidnapped, and there are concrete indications that the kidnapper intends to move the child by car. Police use real-time remote biometric identification in public places to search for that child, within a defined area and for a defined time.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 101, paragraph 332
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.h
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say the police may use real-time identification in this targeted search, which is one of the listed exceptions.

## Notes

The guidelines elsewhere require national law and prior authorisation for any exception; the example does not mention them.
