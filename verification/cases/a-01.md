# a-01

## Description

A video game uses AI with a brain-computer interface: players wear headgear that reads brain activity to control parts of the game. Without the players knowing, the AI trains their brains to give away, through their neural data, very sensitive information such as bank details and intimate facts. This can cause the players significant harm.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 20, paragraph 66
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: red
