# g-07

## Description

An AI system categorises a whole group of people based on biometric data, without looking at or categorising any individual.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 89, paragraph 282
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.g
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say the prohibition does not apply where people are not individually categorised.
