# b-06

## Description

An AI system was trained on biased data and, without anyone intending it, has a disproportionate negative effect on people in a socially or economically disadvantaged situation. It does not deliberately target them. It is not known whether the provider or deployer is aware of the effect and the likely harm, or whether they have taken corrective measures.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 35, paragraph 110
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say such a system should not automatically be considered to exploit vulnerabilities, but it should be if the provider or deployer is aware of the discrimination and the likely significant harm and takes no corrective action, facts the example leaves open.
