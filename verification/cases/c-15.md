# c-15

## Description

An AI system scores legal entities, for example to allocate budget between municipalities. The score is not based on the personal characteristics or social behaviour of individuals, although residents may be affected indirectly.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 58, paragraph 173
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say scoring of legal entities on that basis is in principle excluded from point (c).
