# d-06

## Description

A private company uses AI to profile its customers in the ordinary course of business, for safety and to protect its finances, for example to detect financial irregularities. It has no law enforcement task and does not aim to predict whether a customer will commit a specific crime.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 68, paragraph 210
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such activities should not be considered to fall under point (d).
