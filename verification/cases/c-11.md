# c-11

## Description

A public authority uses an AI system to control fraud in student housing grants. Among its fraud-risk indicators are the applicants' internet connections, family status and level of education, which do not appear relevant or justified for that purpose.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 56, paragraph 168
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines list it as an example of treatment prohibited under both point (c)(i) and (c)(ii).
