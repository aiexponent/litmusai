# a-14

## Description

An AI companion application is designed to seem human and uses affective computing so that users find it appealing and engage more. It does not use other manipulative or deceptive practices that are reasonably likely to cause serious psychological, physical or other harm, unhealthy attachment or dependency.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 44, paragraph 134
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines list it among systems not likely to cause significant harm, which are outside the prohibition.
