# h-13

## Description

During a busy city festival, police use live facial recognition at the entrances, comparing the faces of everyone passing a mobile camera with a watchlist of people wanted for drug trafficking and for sexual offences. The search is broad and untargeted, in the hope of catching wanted people.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 105, paragraph 354
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.h
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say the police are not allowed to deploy real-time facial recognition in this broad, untargeted way, and sexual offences outside the listed ones are not covered.
