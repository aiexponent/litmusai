# ba-05

## Description

A person takes a general-purpose AI image generator that has safeguards and is not meant for intimate content, works around its safeguards, and uses it to create realistic sexually explicit images of an identifiable colleague without that person's consent.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: red
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition on use includes a deployer who circumvents preventive measures or uses for such purposes lawful systems not intended to generate such material.

## Notes

Recital 12 states this for both non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material, so it gives one case for each point (see SELECTION.md).
