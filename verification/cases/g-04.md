# g-04

## Description

An online social network offers filters that categorise users' facial or body features so they can add to or change their photos and videos. The filters work only within the network's content-sharing service.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 88, paragraph 280
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.g
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this can be considered an ancillary feature and is permitted.
