# f-04

## Description

An AI system monitors professional pilots or drivers to detect fatigue, alerts them and suggests when to take breaks to avoid accidents.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 79, paragraph 249
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say fatigue is a physical state, not an emotion, so this is not emotion recognition.

## Notes

Paragraph 261 repeats this example; it is not a separate case.
