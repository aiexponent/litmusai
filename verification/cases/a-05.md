# a-05

## Description

A general-purpose generative AI chatbot sometimes produces false or made-up answers (hallucinations) without anyone intending it. The provider tells users clearly about these limits and has built in safeguards to reduce such outputs. The chatbot is not meant for, and is not used in, sensitive areas such as health, education or elections.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 23, paragraph 73
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such a system may not be considered to deploy deceptive techniques in these circumstances, so a required condition is missing.
