# a-07

## Description

An AI system is deployed to impersonate other people, so that the person talking to it believes they are dealing with someone else. The people are in fact deceived about who they are dealing with. Nothing is said about whether this leads to harm.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 25, paragraph 82
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say such a system is likely to fall within the prohibition if the other conditions, in particular significant harm, are fulfilled, and that this needs a case-by-case assessment.
