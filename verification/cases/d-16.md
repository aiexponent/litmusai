# d-16

## Description

A tax or customs authority uses AI to analyse transaction data, tax declarations and customs data of companies to assess the risk that a company commits tax or customs fraud amounting to a crime.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 71, paragraph 215
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say point (d) applies only to natural persons, typically excluding systems that profile legal entities.
