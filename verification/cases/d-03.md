# d-03

## Description

A police department uses an AI risk tool to assess whether children and teenagers will commit violent and property crimes in future. A child's score depends on their relationships, so a child can be rated high risk just for being linked to a sibling or friend with a high score, and parents' scores also count. High scores lead police to register the children, check on them more and refer them to youth services.
