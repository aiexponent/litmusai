# d-01

## Description

A law enforcement authority uses an AI system to predict who will commit crimes such as terrorism, based only on people's age, nationality, address, type of car and marital status. People are judged more likely to commit future offences purely on these personal characteristics.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 66, paragraph 202
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say such a system may be assumed to be prohibited under point (d).
