# ba-11

## Description

An AI art tool creates stylised, non-realistic nude artworks that do not realistically show any identifiable real person's intimate parts or show them in sexual activity.

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
