# d-11

## Description

Police investigating a possible armed robbery suspect two individuals on the basis of verifiable facts, such as their part in dark-web chats about buying weapons. An AI system combines place-based policing data with number-plate recognition data on the suspects' vehicles to support the officers' assessment.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 70, paragraph 214
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines list it as outside the prohibition because the AI supports a human assessment based on objective, verifiable facts.
