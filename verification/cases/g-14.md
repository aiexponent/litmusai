# g-14

## Description

A law enforcement authority uses AI to label and filter a lawfully held dataset suspected of containing child sexual abuse material: it redacts sensitive data, and filters and labels images by gender, age, eye and hair colour, scars and marks to identify victims, and by features of abusers' hands such as finger length or tattoos to identify suspects.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 90, paragraph 286
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.g
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this labelling and filtering is permitted.
