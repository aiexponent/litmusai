# c-12

## Description

A government runs a comprehensive AI system that monitors and rates citizens on their behaviour across life: social interactions, online activity, purchasing habits and paying bills on time. People with low scores face restricted access to public services, higher loan interest, and difficulty travelling, renting a home or finding a job. Minor infractions lead to heavy social and financial penalties.
