# a-13

## Description

An advertiser uses AI to personalise ads based on users' preferences. It uses no subliminal, manipulative or deceptive techniques and does not play on anyone's vulnerabilities. It follows data protection, consumer protection and digital services rules.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 43, paragraph 133
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such advertising is not inherently manipulative and is a common, legitimate commercial practice.
