# c-25

## Description

An online shopping platform uses AI to score users and gives those with a strong purchase history and few returns perks such as faster returns or refunds without sending items back. Other users keep the standard returns process.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 60, paragraph 177
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this is out of scope of point (c), as the advantages are justified and proportionate.
