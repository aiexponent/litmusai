# ba-16

## Description

An AI editing tool takes an existing intimate image of an identifiable person and changes it to reveal more of their intimate parts, without that person's consent.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: red
- Reason: Recital 12 says any manipulation that increases the level of exposure of depicted intimate parts falls under the prohibition.

## Notes

The recital speaks of increased exposure; the absence of consent is added from point (ba) itself, which the recital applies.
