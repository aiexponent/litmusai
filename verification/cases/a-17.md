# a-17

## Description

An organisation uses AI-driven security training that imitates phishing attempts to teach staff about cybersecurity threats. The simulations may use manipulative techniques, such as playing on cognitive biases, without the users knowing at the time. This is temporary and serves training and awareness.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 44, paragraph 134
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines list it among systems not likely to cause significant harm, which are outside the prohibition.
