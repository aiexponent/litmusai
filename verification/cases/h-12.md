# h-12

## Description

Police are told that a man is running around a park looking for people to attack with a knife while shouting violent extremist slogans. They want to use real-time remote biometric identification in and around the park to identify and locate him and prevent the attack. It is not known whether national law authorises such use.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 104, paragraph 347
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.h
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say police may use the system if the Member State has authorised this use and the other conditions of Article 5(2) to (7) are met, facts the example does not give.
