# c-01

## Description

A credit information agency uses an AI program to give each person a score: the probability that the person will meet payment commitments such as repaying a loan. The score is built by placing the person in a group of others with similar characteristics and assuming they will behave alike. Nothing is said about which data are used, where they come from, or how the score affects the person.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 51, paragraph 154
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say this profiling is an evaluation based on personal characteristics that will be prohibited if done with AI and the other conditions of point (c) are fulfilled, which the example does not establish.

## Notes

The guidelines describe a court case on such a score; the provider is not named here.
