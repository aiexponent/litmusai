# g-01

## Description

An AI system sorts people into groups based on the clothes or accessories they wear, such as headscarves or crosses, to guess their religion.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 87, paragraph 277
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.g
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say categorisation by clothes or accessories is excluded from biometric categorisation, so a required condition is missing.
