# a-03

## Description

A general AI assistant now and then shows manipulative behaviour that its provider did not design or intend. The behaviour is incidental. The provider has assessed the risk of significant harm and has put in place appropriate measures to prevent and reduce it.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 21, paragraph 69
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say merely incidental manipulative behaviour is not a purposefully manipulative technique as long as the provider has taken appropriate preventive and mitigating measures, so a required condition is missing.
