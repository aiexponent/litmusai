# c-14

## Description

A private credit agency uses an AI system to judge people's creditworthiness and decide whether they get a housing loan, based on personal characteristics unrelated to that decision.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 57, paragraph 170
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines call it an unacceptable social scoring practice by a private actor.
