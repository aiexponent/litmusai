# a-16

## Description

An online music service uses emotion recognition to infer users' moods and recommend songs that fit them, while avoiding heavy exposure to depressing songs. Users are simply listening to music and are not led into depression or anxiety.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 44, paragraph 134
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say the system is not reasonably likely to cause significant harm, so it is outside the prohibition.

## Notes

The same example could raise point (f) questions, but the setting is consumers, not a workplace or school.
