# b-03

## Description

An AI system targets older people with deceptive personalised offers and scams. It plays on their reduced cognitive capacity to push them into decisions they would not otherwise take and that are likely to cause them significant financial harm.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 34, paragraph 106
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say point (b) targets such exploitative practices that are likely to seriously harm older persons.
