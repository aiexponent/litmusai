# bb-03

## Description

A provider offers an AI image generator that, by its design and training, can readily and repeatedly be made to produce realistic sexual images of children from simple prompts, without technical changes. It has no reasonable or adequate safeguards to prevent this and does not act on reported misuse.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.bb
- Expected label: red
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition covers systems where such output is a reasonably foreseeable and reproducible outcome and there are no reasonable and adequate safeguards.

## Notes

Recital 12 states this for both non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material, so it gives one case for each point (see SELECTION.md).
