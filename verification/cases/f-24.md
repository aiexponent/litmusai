# f-24

## Description

An employer or an education institution uses an AI system intended to detect burnout or depression among its staff or students.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 83, paragraph 257
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say this is not covered by the medical exception and remains prohibited.
