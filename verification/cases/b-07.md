# b-07

## Description

A company uses AI to target consumers based on wide-ranging variables such as the brand and model of their phone, the size of the city they live in and how much and where they travel.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 36, paragraph 111
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say point (b) does not apply to targeting based on such variables, which do not identify people in a specific social or economic situation.
