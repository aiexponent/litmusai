# bb-08

## Description

A national police authority, acting under its domestic legal powers in criminal proceedings, uses an AI system to generate or manipulate child sexual abuse material in order to investigate crime. National law gives a 'without right' defence for this activity.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 13
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.bb
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 13 says the prohibition should not prevent such use where a 'without right' defence applies under national law, naming activities by authorities to conduct criminal proceedings or to prevent, detect or investigate crime.
