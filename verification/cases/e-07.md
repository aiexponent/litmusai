# e-07

## Description

A developer gathers a large set of facial images to train and test AI models. The database is not used to recognise or identify anyone.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 76, paragraph 234
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.e
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say facial image databases not used for recognising people, such as training or test sets, are out of scope.
