# c-05

## Description

A social welfare agency uses an AI system to estimate how likely recipients of household allowances are to commit fraud. It relies on characteristics taken from contexts with no apparent link to fraud, such as having a spouse of a certain nationality or ethnic origin, having an internet connection, behaviour on social platforms, or performance at work.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 54, paragraph 166
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines list it as treatment in unrelated social contexts prohibited under point (c)(i).
