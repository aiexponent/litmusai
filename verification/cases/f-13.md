# f-13

## Description

A supermarket or bank uses AI cameras to spot suspicious customers, for example to conclude that someone is about to commit a robbery. It is ensured that no employees are tracked, and there are sufficient safeguards.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 82, paragraph 254
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this is not prohibited under point (f) in these conditions.
