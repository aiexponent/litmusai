# c-09

## Description

A municipality uses an AI system to score residents' trustworthiness from many data points on their social behaviour in different contexts, including too little volunteering and minor lapses such as returning library books late, putting out rubbish on the wrong day or paying local taxes late. Residents scored as less trustworthy are blacklisted: they lose public benefits, face other serious penalties and are watched more closely.
