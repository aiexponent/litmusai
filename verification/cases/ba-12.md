# ba-12

## Description

A clothing retailer's AI try-on app lets a customer see garments on a realistic image of their own body. Intimate parts are not exposed, and where any would be, the depicted person has given free, specific, informed, unambiguous and explicit consent.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition does not cover generative applications where intimate parts are not exposed or, if exposed, the depicted person has given explicit consent, naming try-on applications.
