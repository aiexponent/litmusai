# c-19

## Description

A company uses AI to evaluate customers for financial fraud, using relevant data such as transaction behaviour and metadata within its own services, past history and other sources objectively relevant to fraud risk. Any detrimental treatment is justified and proportionate to fraudulent behaviour.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 59, paragraph 177
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such practices are not affected by the prohibition.
