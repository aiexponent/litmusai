# h-08

## Description

A bank uses remote biometric identification on its own behalf to detect and counter crimes such as fraud or money laundering. It is not acting on the instructions of law enforcement.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 99, paragraph 324
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.h
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say entities acting on their own behalf in this way are not considered to fall under point (h).
