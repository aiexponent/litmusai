# a-06

## Description

A chatbot that can be used in many settings is designed to show advertisements that flash brief visual cues and carry inaudible audio signals, and to play on users' emotional dependency. The design aims to steer purchasing decisions without users being aware of it, pushing people into financial decisions that cause them significant harm.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 25, paragraph 82
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say this is a technique deployed with the objective of materially distorting behaviour and that, with the significant harm present, the system is likely to fall within the prohibition.

## Notes

The guidelines add that a case-by-case assessment is needed. The example itself states the significant financial harm, so it is read as red.
