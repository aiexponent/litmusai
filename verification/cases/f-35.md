# f-35

## Description

A system at a football stadium or a central station recognises aggressive behaviour in the crowd. It may also pick up the emotions of security staff working there, although the deployer has safeguards to avoid screening employees and the system is not aimed at them.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 86, paragraph 270
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such systems should be considered outside the prohibition since their primary objective is not assessing employees' emotions.
