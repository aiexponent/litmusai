# a-15

## Description

A therapeutic chatbot uses subliminal techniques to steer users toward a healthier lifestyle and help them quit habits such as smoking. Users who follow it may feel some temporary physical discomfort and stress from the effort of quitting. There is no hidden aim beyond promoting healthy habits.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 44, paragraph 134
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this chatbot cannot be considered likely to cause significant harm, so it is outside the prohibition.
