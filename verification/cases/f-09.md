# f-09

## Description

An employer uses an AI system that monitors the emotional tone of hybrid work teams by inferring emotions from the voices and video of their online calls, to foster social awareness and prevent conflict.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 81, paragraph 254
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say such systems are prohibited.
