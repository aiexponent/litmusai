# g-03

## Description

An online marketplace offers a filter that categorises a shopper's facial or body features so the shopper can preview a product on themselves. The filter works only as part of selling the product.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 88, paragraph 280
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.g
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this use is permitted under point (g), as an ancillary feature.
