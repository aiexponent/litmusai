# h-14

## Description

Police have a photo and description of one person wanted on a European Arrest Warrant for drug trafficking and reason to believe he will be at a city festival. They use real-time facial recognition at the festival to identify that one person.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 106, paragraph 354
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.h
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this may be covered by the exception in point (h)(iii).
