# d-02

## Description

A national tax authority runs an AI tool over all tax returns to predict possible criminal tax offences and pick cases for investigation. It works only from a profile built by the AI, using traits such as dual nationality, place of birth and number of children, and opaque inferred variables that are hard to verify. There is no reasonable suspicion or other objective, verifiable fact linking a person to a crime.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 66, paragraph 202
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say such a system will normally fall under the prohibition in point (d).

## Notes

The guidelines add that it is also social scoring under point (c); the target here is (d).
