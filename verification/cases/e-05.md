# e-05

## Description

An AI system collects voice recordings from across the internet in an untargeted way to build a database of voice samples for identifying speakers. It collects no facial images.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 76, paragraph 234
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.e
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say the prohibition does not apply to untargeted scraping of biometric data other than facial images.
