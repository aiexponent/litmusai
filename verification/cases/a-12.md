# a-12

## Description

AI-enabled subliminal techniques are used in the psychological treatment of a mental illness and in physical rehabilitation. The use follows the applicable law and medical standards, and the patient or their legal representative gives explicit consent.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 43, paragraph 132
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines, following recital 29, say the prohibitions do not affect such practices in medical treatment.
