# ba-14

## Description

In an emergency, medical professionals use an AI system that generates nude images of an unconscious patient's intimate parts for diagnosis and treatment. The patient cannot consent, and the use follows fundamental rights law, data protection law and medical law.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition does not preclude this exceptional use for medical diagnosis and treatment where the person is incapable of consent.
