# e-02

## Description

A company builds a facial recognition database step by step by scraping faces from the internet and CCTV footage, each time choosing a specific group of people or another criterion. Taken together, the result is the same as collecting faces indiscriminately from the start.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 75, paragraph 230
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.e
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say this should fall within the prohibition where the end result is functionally the same as untargeted scraping.
