# c-23

## Description

A retailer uses AI to target commercial advertising based on relevant data such as users' preferences, in line with consumer protection, data protection and digital services law. It does not lead to unfair treatment such as exploitative differential pricing.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 60, paragraph 177
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such targeted advertising is out of scope of point (c).
