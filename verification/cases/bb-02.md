# bb-02

## Description

A provider offers an AI application whose purpose is to generate realistic sexual images of children.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.bb
- Expected label: red
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition covers systems intended to generate or manipulate such material.

## Notes

Recital 12 states this for both non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material, so it gives one case for each point (see SELECTION.md).
