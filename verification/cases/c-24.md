# c-24

## Description

An AI system uses data collected in refugee camps, such as compliance with camp rules, to support decisions on resettlement or employment. The data is relevant to that assessment and the procedures of EU migration law are followed so that any treatment is justified and proportionate.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 60, paragraph 177
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this is not affected by the prohibition.
