# c-13

## Description

A life insurer collects spending and other financial information from a bank, information that is not relevant to deciding who is eligible for life insurance. An AI system analyses it and recommends whether to refuse a contract or charge higher premiums to an individual or a group of customers.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 57, paragraph 170
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines call it an unacceptable social scoring practice by a private actor.
