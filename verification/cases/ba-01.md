# ba-01

## Description

A provider offers a general-purpose AI image and video generator to the public. It is not intended to produce intimate images of real people. The provider has state-of-the-art safeguards that reliably prevent such output, including cleaned training data, refusal training, prompt and output filters, abuse detection, usage restrictions and a notice-and-action process, and it corrects reported misuse and circumvention.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 says the prohibition should not prevent providers from developing the technical capability to generate images, and covers providers only where the system is intended for such material or lacks reasonable and adequate safeguards.

## Notes

Recital 12 states this for both non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material, so it gives one case for each point (see SELECTION.md).
