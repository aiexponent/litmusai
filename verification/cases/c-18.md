# c-18

## Description

A lender or credit information agency uses an AI credit scoring system to assess a customer's creditworthiness and outstanding debts. It relies on the customer's income, expenses and other financial circumstances that are relevant to that purpose, and follows consumer protection law on the data used and on fair treatment.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 59, paragraph 177
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines list it among legitimate scoring practices outside point (c).
