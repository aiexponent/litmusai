# h-04

## Description

A face identification system controls entry to a restricted area such as a power plant. People present themselves to the entrance camera, and the system compares their face with a database of people authorised to enter.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 94, paragraph 305
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say identity checks for access to premises are excluded from the concept of 'remote', so a required condition is missing.
