# c-20

## Description

A car insurer offering telematics-based tariffs uses data from a device in the car showing that a policyholder speeds or drives unsafely, and raises that policyholder's premium in proportion to the higher accident risk.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 59, paragraph 177
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines list it among legitimate scoring practices outside point (c), provided the increase is proportionate, which the example states.
