# a-10

## Description

A company uses an AI system that analyses customers' emotions to improve customer service and support. It works openly: customers know about it, and it is used in their interest.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 42, paragraph 129
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this system engages in persuasion that aligns with users' interests, which is outside the prohibition.
