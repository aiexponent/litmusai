# b-15

## Description

A bank uses AI for services such as mortgages and loans that takes a client's age or socio-economic situation as an input. It is designed to protect and support people identified as vulnerable and benefits them, and it follows financial services, consumer protection, data protection and non-discrimination law.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 43, paragraph 133
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such systems do not qualify as exploitation of vulnerabilities under point (b).
