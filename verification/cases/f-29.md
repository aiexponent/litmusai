# f-29

## Description

A retailer uses emotion recognition on customers in online marketing, based on their typing or voice messages to virtual assistants, to show personalised messages and ads, including on smart billboards.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 85, paragraph 266
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say emotion recognition used commercially on customers does not fall under point (f).

## Notes

The guidelines note it could still fall under points (a) or (b) if their conditions are met.
