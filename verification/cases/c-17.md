# c-17

## Description

An online ride-sharing or holiday-rental platform shows ratings of drivers and hosts that are simply the sum of individual ratings given by users. The ratings are not combined with other information or analysed by AI to evaluate the people.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 58, paragraph 174
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines distinguish such user ratings from AI social scoring and say they do not necessarily involve AI.
