# b-02

## Description

A video game for children uses AI to analyse each child's behaviour and preferences and creates personalised, unpredictable rewards with addictive reinforcement loops to drive excessive, compulsive play. It is designed to be highly addictive and plays on children's limited self-control and grasp of long-term consequences. This can seriously harm their health, sleep, concentration, schooling and development.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 33, paragraph 105
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.b
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines give this as exploitation and addiction-like practice that seriously harms children, which they say point (b) targets.
