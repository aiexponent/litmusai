# d-04

## Description

A law enforcement authority asks a private company that sells AI crime analytics software to analyse large amounts of data from many sources, such as national registers, bank transactions, communications and location data, to predict which individuals may commit human trafficking offences.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 68, paragraph 208
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: amber
- Reason: The guidelines say such a use case could be prohibited if all the criteria of point (d) are met, which the example does not settle.
