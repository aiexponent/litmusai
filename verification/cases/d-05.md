# d-05

## Description

A bank must screen and profile customers for money laundering under EU anti-money-laundering law and uses an AI system to do it. The system uses only the objective, verifiable data the law specifies, and every prediction is checked by a human as that law requires.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 68, paragraph 209
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say compliance with that law means the use falls outside point (d).
