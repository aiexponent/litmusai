# c-06

## Description

A social welfare agency uses an AI system to estimate the risk of fraud by benefit recipients, using only data that is relevant to awarding the benefit and that was lawfully collected for that purpose. The aim is to check that benefits go to the right people.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 55, paragraph 166
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.c
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such relevant, lawfully collected data could be used to determine the risk of fraud, as the authority pursues a legitimate aim.
