# f-31

## Description

A crowd control system at a football match or concert infers individuals' emotions, for example by counting angry faces in the crowd. It is aimed at spectators, not at a workplace or school.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 85, paragraph 268
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such systems would normally not fall under the prohibition, as they are typically not used in the workplace or education.
