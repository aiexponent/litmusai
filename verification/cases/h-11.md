# h-11

## Description

Authorities use real-time remote biometric identification to search for a missing vulnerable person under an administrative procedure. There is no suspicion of a crime or other law enforcement purpose.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 101, paragraph 336
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.h
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this would not be for law enforcement purposes and so falls under data protection rules instead of point (h).
