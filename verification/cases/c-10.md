# c-10

## Description

A tax authority uses an AI system to detect child benefit fraud. It profiles recipients and sorts suspected ones into categories such as 'deliberate intent or gross negligence' using criteria such as low income, dual nationality and social behaviour. On the basis of the risk score, files are inspected, benefits are often stopped, repayment is demanded and standard debt arrangements are refused, driving many families into severe financial hardship.
