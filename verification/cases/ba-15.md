# ba-15

## Description

An AI editing tool is applied to an existing intimate image to change the background, add a text heading, or enhance contrast or brightness. It does not show more of any intimate part than before and does not change the nature of any sexual activity shown.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 and Article 5(1b) say such manipulation, which does not increase exposure, is not manipulation for point (ba).
