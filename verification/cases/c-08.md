# c-08

## Description

A public agency uses an AI system to profile families for early detection of children at risk. It uses criteria such as parents' mental health and unemployment, and also information on parents' social behaviour drawn from many contexts. Families with high scores are singled out for inspection, and children are taken from their families even for minor lapses by the parents, such as occasionally missing a doctor's appointment or getting a traffic fine.
