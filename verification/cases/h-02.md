# h-02

## Description

Police use a remote biometric identification system after the event, on recorded footage, to identify people retrospectively. It is not used live.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 91, paragraph 292
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.h
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such non-real-time use is not prohibited, though it is subject to extra safeguards.
