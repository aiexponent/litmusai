# e-03

## Description

An AI tool that feeds a facial recognition database runs targeted searches for images of particular people and, alongside them, untargeted searches that pull in any facial images it finds on the internet.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 75, paragraph 231
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.e
- Expected label: red
- Reason: The guidelines say that where systems combine targeted and untargeted searches, the untargeted scraping is prohibited.
