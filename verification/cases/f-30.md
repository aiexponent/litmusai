# f-30

## Description

A crowd control system at a large event analyses the general noise and mood level in a place, without inferring the emotions of any individual person.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 85, paragraph 267
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say this does not fall within point (f) because it does not infer emotions of a natural person.
