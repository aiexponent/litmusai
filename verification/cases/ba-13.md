# ba-13

## Description

A medical AI application produces anatomical simulations and analyses mammograms that show patients' intimate parts. Each patient gives free, specific, informed, unambiguous and explicit consent.

## Source

- Document: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), 8 July 2026, OJ L 2026/1744, 24.7.2026
- Location: recital 12
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.ba
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: Recital 12 names medical applications such as anatomical simulations and mammograms, with the person's explicit consent, as not covered.
