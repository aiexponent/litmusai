# a-02

## Description

A video game lets players control parts of the game with headgear that reads brain activity, using AI. The interface is designed to be safe and secure, it respects the players' privacy and autonomy, and it does not try to extract information or influence players without their knowledge.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 20, paragraph 66
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.a
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say the prohibition does not target brain-computer interface applications in general when they are designed safely and respect privacy and autonomy.
