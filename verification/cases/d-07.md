# d-07

## Description

A police force uses an AI predictive policing system that scores the likelihood of crimes such as burglary or knife crime in different areas of a city, using past crime rates by area and street maps, to decide where to send more or fewer patrols. It does not assess individuals.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 69, paragraph 212
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.d
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say location-based crime prediction falls outside the prohibition.
