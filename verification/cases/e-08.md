# e-08

## Description

An AI system harvests large numbers of facial images from the internet to train a model that generates new images of fictitious people. It is not used to recognise real people.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 76, paragraph 235
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.e
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say the prohibition does not apply because such systems do not result in recognition of real persons.
