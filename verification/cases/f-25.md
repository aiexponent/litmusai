# f-25

## Description

An employer or school uses emotion recognition for medical reasons, to assist employees or students with autism and to improve accessibility for people who are blind or deaf.

## Source

- Document: Commission Guidelines on prohibited AI practices, C(2025) 5052 final, 29 July 2025
- Location: page 84, paragraph 263
- Read on: 2026-11-02

## Expected

- Target point: 5.1.f
- Expected label: clear
- Reason: The guidelines say such uses fall within the exception for medical reasons.

## Notes

The guidelines read therapeutic uses as uses of CE-marked medical devices (paragraph 257); the example does not say whether the device is CE-marked.
